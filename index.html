HOW THE DEMO WORKS

From disconnected data to decisions.

This local prototype simulates the reporting process. It does not contact QuickBooks, Clover or Claude.

01

QuickBooks product reference

105 real product SKUs and names extracted from the supplied QuickBooks sales report. This is a reference list—not purchasing data.

Preloaded · supplied source
02

Generated Clover-style sales

116 simulated item-sales records across 72 SKUs. Values reproduce the mock presentation's headline totals, with synthetic product distributions and dates.

Preloaded · synthetic example
03

Deterministic SKU comparison

The application compares product identifiers, calculates revenue totals and keeps unmatched items in a separate review queue.

Working JavaScript · no AI required
04

Dashboard and export

Results immediately appear on every screen. Demonstrate fixes to the unmatched queue or download Excel-compatible CSV data.

Working interface · offline
